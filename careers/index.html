Why Karsha

Three reasons people stay.

  • 01

    The real price is a culture

    Every fill is auditable and every fee is published. People who like being checked stay a long time.

  • 02

    Small teams, big surfaces

    Four people own the matching engine. Three own custody. You will see your work on the screen within a month.

  • 03

    Lisbon, Zurich, anywhere

    Core trading and custody sit together. Everything else is remote by default, with two team weeks a year.

Benefits, every role

  • Salary bands published inside the company
  • Equity for every full-time role
  • Private health and dental, family included
  • 30 days leave, plus the week between the years
  • €3,000 a year for learning
  • Hardware of your choice, replaced every three years
